Mega Power Arcade Privacy Policy

COAF Research. Last updated 4 November 2026.

Mega Power Arcade is a collection of games for Android sold by COAF Research. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: it collects nothing and sends nothing.

What the app collects

Nothing. Mega Power Arcade has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchases. It does not collect, store or share personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos or usage data. It does not use the camera or the microphone.

Internet use

The games work offline. The only network activity is the Google Play Store delivering the app and, for the game Cat Ninja, an optional one-off download of about 520 MB of game data through Google Play when that game is first opened. That download is handled by Google Play; COAF Research runs no servers and receives no data from it.

Cat Ninja and the on-device language model

Cat Ninja uses a small language model to write what its village characters say and to turn a typed weapon idea into a weapon design. The model runs entirely on your device. Nothing you type is sent anywhere, and nothing is kept once the game closes.

Game progress

Some games save progress, such as levels reached or items collected, in the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves the device and is removed when the app is uninstalled.

Children

Mega Power Arcade is suitable for children. Because the app collects no information at all, it collects no information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

Contact
